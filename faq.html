FAQ

Questions before you enquire.

Clear answers help actors decide whether Access is the right fit for their current screen work.

Do I need acting experience?

Experience helps, though the central requirement is seriousness. Access is suitable for emerging actors, trained actors, working actors, and returning actors who want clear screen craft and direct notes.

Is this theatre training or screen training?

Access is screen-focused. The work uses acting technique, scene analysis, camera practice, playback, and adjustment for the frame. The same techniques can serve stage work, while the studio applies them primarily through screen practice.

Will I be filmed?

Where useful, yes. Filming and playback are central tools. Actors learn through camera evidence, playback, notes, and the next adjustment.

Do I receive footage?

For coaching, footage use depends on the session purpose. For coaching work, usable clips are part of the agreed scope. Public use, scene partner consent, and edit delivery are confirmed before filming.

How direct are the notes?

The notes are direct, specific, and practical. The aim is to clarify the work, sharpen choices, and help the actor make a stronger adjustment in the next take.

Can beginners enquire?

Beginners can enquire. Access may recommend a foundations-focused coaching session before audition or self-tape work.

Can you help with auditions and self-tapes?

Yes. Coaching can cover text analysis, playable choices, tone, eyeline, camera setup, redirects, and final performance adjustment.

Where does coaching happen?

Coaching can happen in Sydney or online, depending on the need, the scene, and the filming requirements. Exact location and preparation details are confirmed directly.

How do I enquire?

Use the Apply page. Send your contact details, experience, what you need, any deadline, and links to existing material. Access will recommend the most suitable next step.
