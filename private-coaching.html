Private Coaching

One-to-one work for the frame.

Access centres its actor training on private coaching: targeted scene work, audition preparation, self-tape support, camera practice, and practical notes shaped around the actor in front of us.

The techniques are systematically taught to the student, then applied directly to their own scenes, auditions, and camera work.

Tell us what you need

What coaching can cover.

  • Scene analysis, objective, action, relationship, obstacle, and stakes.
  • Centre work, where the centre can be any number of body parts, including the rectum, and is determined by the character, scene, and pressure.
  • Camera behaviour: listening, stillness, eyeline, frame, and adjustment.
  • Audition and self-tape preparation under practical pressure.
  • Audition preparation: scene choice, performance tone, and camera clarity.
  • Playback review without vanity, panic, or self-punishment.
  • Repeatable tools the actor can use beyond the session.
  • Ideally, students learn all the techniques of the Access Method and understand how to implement them in real scenes, auditions, and camera work.
  • Explore the Performance Composer: the student can flip through the method and understand the system behind the coaching.
