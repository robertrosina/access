The Access Method

Truthful behaviour shaped with purpose.

Actors learn to access truthful behaviour and shape it with purpose in service of the story.

Open the Workbook See Classes

High-level technique

The actor moves from receiving to shaping.

The Access Method treats acting as a chain of clear events: receive the script, receive the partner, form a point of view, pursue a want, meet an obstacle, and shape behaviour so the audience receives the story.

The method gives the actor concrete handles for invisible work. A feeling becomes a playable action. A thought becomes a line, look, breath, movement, silence, or change. A relationship becomes pressure in the body, voice, eyes, timing, and frame.

Each scene is prepared through craft and then tested through camera evidence: filmed takes, playback, notes, adjustment, and repeatable rehearsal.

How the method works

A practical path through the scene.

01

Receive

The actor lets words, given circumstances, images, memories, partner behaviour, and story pressure land before solving the scene.

02

Choose

The actor defines want, obstacle, relationship, status, stakes, and the change the character is trying to create.

03

Shape

The actor turns the work into thought, movement, action, voice, eye behaviour, physical pressure, mask, and tactic.

04

Test

The actor rehearses on camera, watches what reads, sharpens what disappears, and adjusts with discipline.

05

Repeat

The actor builds a usable map of beats, anchors, technique choices, rehearsal runs, and playback notes that can be returned to under pressure.
