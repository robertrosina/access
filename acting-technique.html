What the course covers.

  • How to identify the playable problem of a scene.
  • How objective, relationship, and obstacle create behaviour.
  • How stakes change the pressure of a moment.
  • How the camera reads stillness, thought, listening, and change.
  • How to use playback without vanity, panic, or self-punishment.
  • How to make choices that create an audience effect.
