Founders

Built by screen people.

Access Acting Studio was founded by David Barry and Robert Rosina to give actors a practical, screen-focused room built around craft, camera evidence, and story service.

Founder

David Barry

David Barry is a screen actor and teacher whose work at Access Acting Studio supports practical actor training, camera awareness, scene preparation, and disciplined rehearsal.

He brings professional experience into the room through direct feedback, structured exercises, and a clear focus on what actors need when working on camera.

His teaching helps actors listen, adjust, repeat, and make choices that remain alive within the frame.

Founder

Robert Rosina

Robert Rosina brings film production training, extensive acting technique study, professional strategic communication, and production-led storytelling to Access.

His teaching focus is practical: scene analysis, behaviour, audience effect, camera evidence, and a repeatable method actors can use under pressure.

Robert’s core principle for the school is simple: actors learn to access truthful behaviour and shape it with purpose in service of the story.
