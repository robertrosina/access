Privacy

Privacy policy.

Access collects the information needed to answer enquiries, assess fit, manage bookings, and deliver coaching and filmed review work.

Information collected.

Access may collect your name, contact details, acting experience, training goals, links to performance materials, coaching interest, payment status, and messages you send to the studio.

When work is filmed, footage may be used for teaching, review, or actor development. Public use requires permission from the relevant participants.

UseStudio administration

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess fit, manage bookings, and support actor development.

Actor information is kept with reasonable care and accessed only for studio purposes.
