Privacy

Privacy policy.

Access collects the information needed to answer enquiries, assess class suitability, manage enrolments, and deliver training.

Information collected.

Access may collect your name, contact details, acting experience, training goals, links to performance materials, class interest, payment status, and messages you send to the studio.

When classes are filmed, footage may be used for teaching, review, and actor development inside the studio context. Public use requires permission from the relevant participants.

UseStudio administration

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess applications, manage classes, and support student training.

Student information is kept with reasonable care and accessed only for studio purposes.
