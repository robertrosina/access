Screen Coaching

Camera work with real feedback.

Private screen coaching brings the work into direct focus: scene work, camera practice, playback, specific notes, adjustment, and repeatable craft.

What happens in the session.

  • The actor works on selected scenes, auditions, or short exercises.
  • Takes are filmed, reviewed, and adjusted where useful.
  • Notes focus on what the camera receives.
  • The actor builds truthful behaviour under repeatable conditions.
  • The actor learns how to take direction without collapsing the work.
