The Access Method

Truthful behaviour, shaped with purpose.

The Access Method is the teaching spine of the school: practical technique, professional screen experience, scene work, camera practice, playback, and purposeful adjustment.

Actors learn to stop guessing, build behaviour with structure, test choices on camera, and refine what lands in service of the story.

TRUTH

Access truthful behaviour

Become more available, responsive, connected, and believable under imaginary circumstances.

CHOICE

Shape purposeful choices

Use objective, relationship, action, obstacle, stakes, and adjustment to build the scene.

STORY

Serve the story

Make choices that move the scene, affect the other person, and clarify the work.

REFINE

Test it on camera

Use diagnostic filming and playback to see what reads, what lands, and what needs adjustment.

Professional Acting Authority

He is best known for his standout ongoing role as Lord John Grey in the global series Outlander and as James Bligh in the award-winning series A Place to Call Home. He brings students a working actor’s understanding of preparation, continuity, adjustment, camera discipline, professional screen standards, and story service.

Head of Voice

Daniel Belle is a musical theatre and concert performer with credits across Opera Australia, Les Misérables, My Fair Lady, Cinderella, and The Ten Tenors. As Head of Voice, he helps actors build vocal presence, breath, resonance, phrasing, musicality, stamina, and confidence inside performance.

Film Production Authority

Robert Rosina brings a production-focused approach to actor training, shaped by a degree in filmmaking, broad study of acting technique, and professional experience in strategic communication. Launch course

Access Weekend Intensive

First intake Sat 12 – Sun 13 September 2026 NIDA Studios, Sydney • 2 days • 8–12 actors

Spend one weekend on camera. Learn the foundations of The Access Method, rehearse a scene, film it, review playback, receive professional notes, and leave with training footage for private review.

What you will do

  • Work through The Access Method framework.
  • Complete camera basics, diagnostic filming, and first playback review.
  • Build truthful behaviour through listening, receiving, response, and connection.
  • Shape choices with objective, relationship, action, obstacle, and stakes.
  • Rehearse, film, review playback, adjust, and receive next-step guidance.
Duration 2 days
Teaching time 12 hours
Class size 8-12 actors
Focus Technique, camera, playback, filmed scene
Footage Training footage for private review
Next step Private coaching + Screen Acting Foundations

Diagnostic filming is a learning baseline: evidence, not judgment. Showreel-level filmed scenes may be offered later as a separate premium product.
