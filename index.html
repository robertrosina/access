First Response Notes

Ignore the script's “big text” commentary

Use the script for facts, actions, relationships, and spoken evidence. I recommend ignoring the script's broad “big text” commentary about how the character feels, behaves, or delivers the scene. The director will decide those matters. In the absence of a director telling you otherwise, be your own director and choose the interpretation that best serves the story.
