Actor Development

A serious route through screen craft.

Longer development is shaped as a private coaching plan for actors who want continuity, clear standards, and a way to measure growth on camera.

The development sequence.

  • Foundations: behaviour, objective, action, relationship, obstacle, and stakes.
  • Camera craft: frame, continuity, marks, eyeline, and adjustment.
  • Scene study: story pressure, playable choices, rhythm, and audience effect.
  • Audition work: self-tapes, redirects, preparation, and professional discipline.
  • Audition application: material that shows truthful behaviour, camera life, and playable choices.
